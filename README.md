## 📌 Project Overview

Insurance costs can vary across individuals due to differences in customer characteristics and other factors. This project uses **Python and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to examine an insurance dataset, understand its structure, investigate patterns in insurance charges, and communicate findings through data visualization.

The project demonstrates a practical data analytics workflow, combining data inspection, statistical exploration, visualization, and interpretation to transform raw data into meaningful insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📂 Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset.
- 🧹 Inspect data quality, data types, missing values, and potential inconsistencies.
- 📊 Explore distributions and descriptive statistics of insurance charges.
- 🔎 Investigate relationships between customer attributes and insurance expenses.
- 📈 Create visualizations to communicate patterns and comparisons.
- 💡 Summarize evidence-based observations from the analysis.

## 🔍 Analysis Workflow

1. **Data Loading** — Import the dataset and inspect its structure.
2. **Data Quality Assessment** — Examine data types, missing values, and potential inconsistencies.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — Calculate descriptive statistics and examine distributions.
4. **Relationship Analysis** — Explore associations between customer attributes and insurance charges.
5. **Data Visualization** — Use charts to identify and communicate patterns.
6. **Interpretation** — Document findings supported by the analysis.

## 📊 Key Business Questions

- How are insurance charges distributed across customers?
- Which customer characteristics are associated with differences in insurance expenses?
- How do insurance charges vary across different customer groups?
- What patterns and relationships can be identified through exploratory analysis?
- How can data visualization support a clearer understanding of insurance costs?
